# SEO inventory (Phase 0.1)

Full per-route tables are generated by the SEO audit crawler:

- `inventory-before.md`: the site as delivered on 24 Sep 2026 (87 URLs crawled, 84 in the sitemap).
- `inventory-after.md`: this implementation (92 URLs crawled, 83 in the sitemap).

Each row lists status, title, H1, canonical, hreflang, indexability, sitemap membership, word count and JSON-LD types.

To regenerate: `npm run build && npx next start -p 3000 & npm run seo:audit -- --inventory docs/seo/inventory-after.md`

## Routes before the change (24 Sep)

- **Feature pages (10):** `/features/` + fleet-management, fleet-maintenance, vehicle-management, asset-management, expense-management, fuel-management, inspection-management, work-orders, preventive-maintenance, reports-analytics. Each had an Arabic twin at `/ar/features/…`. They had 130–260 words, and most titles ended in "in Egypt".
- **Solution pages (4):** `/solutions/` + fleet-cost-management, fleet-maintenance-management, asset-lifecycle-management, equipment-cost-management.
- **Industry pages (8):** logistics, transportation, construction, manufacturing, real-estate, healthcare, travel-hospitality, energy-utilities.
- **Market hubs:** `/en-eg`, `/en-sa`, `/en-ae`, `/en-qa`, `/en-jo`, `/en-iq`, `/en-mena`, plus `/ar-*` twins.
- **Blog posts (4):** what-is-fleet-management, preventive-vs-reactive-maintenance, how-to-calculate-fleet-cost, what-is-asset-management. They had 92–154 words of body text.
- **Tool:** `/resources/fleet-cost-calculator`, a monthly cost only.

## Implementations found

- **Metadata:** `lib/seo.ts` had a `buildMetadata` / `buildCountryMetadata` helper. Canonicals were absolute on `https://axpense.net`, hreflang was en/ar/x-default, and og:locale was `ar_EG` on every Arabic page.
- **Sitemap:** `app/sitemap.ts` used `lastModified: new Date()` on every URL and had no hreflang alternates.
- **Robots:** `app/robots.ts` allowed everything and disallowed `/admin` and `/api/`.
- **JSON-LD:**
  - Organization and WebSite everywhere.
  - FAQPage on the home and market pages.
  - SoftwareApplication on feature pages, without `offers`.
  - Article on the blog, without `image`.
- **Breadcrumbs:** visible on feature and industry pages only. BreadcrumbList schema was on market pages only.
